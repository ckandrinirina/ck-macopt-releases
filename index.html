ck-macopt

A menu bar app that keeps an Intel MacBook Pro at full speed.

No release yet

What it does

Fan control

Ramps the fans before the CPU gets hot enough to throttle, from the temperature and how fast it is rising.

Throttle guard

Briefly turns on Low Power Mode only when the CPU is about to fall towards 1.2 GHz, and turns it off once it is cool.

Background hogs

Flags apps that sit in the background using a lot of memory or CPU. You decide whether to quit them.

Storage

Shows what is using disk space, folder by folder.

Uninstall

Removes an app together with its leftover files. Everything goes to the Trash, after you confirm the list.

Network

Live download and upload speed, and a speed test.

GPU

Tells you when the discrete GPU is in use.

Startup items

Lists the login items and launch agents that start with your Mac.

Made for Intel MacBook Pros

Fan control and the helper that does it are Intel-only, tuned on a 16-inch MacBook Pro (2019). On another Intel Mac only the sensors it finds are used. It is read-only on Apple Silicon: it shows the live stats and says that fan control is not supported.

It needs macOS 14 or later.

Opening it the first time

ck-macopt is signed with a self-signed certificate and is not notarized, so macOS asks before it opens the app. Unzip it, move ck-macopt.app to Applications, then either:

  1. Right-click the app and choose Open, then click Open again in the dialog.
  2. Or open it once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the message about ck-macopt and click Open Anyway.

You only do this once. After that ck-macopt updates itself in the background.
